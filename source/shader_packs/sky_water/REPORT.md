## 3. Water: what the lite recipe keeps and loses

Each water plan is **one texture + prebaked fresnel + one constant highlight**:

1. stage 0 multiplies the base/scene texture by a prebaked fresnel tint
   (vertex colour, or material constant `c0` when the IR has no colour
   varying);
2. stage 1 adds one constant sun highlight;
3. a final REPLACE stage forces alpha to 1 as in the retail epilogue.

**Dropped, and named per hash:**

* the reflection render target and the refraction pair;
* the per-pixel normal map and normal perturbation;
* the `dp3`/`rcp`/`pow` fresnel and specular terms;
* the 2-3 light loop / `rep`/`cmp` selection when present;
* the 4-tap normal blur (`rcp`/`frc`/`cmp`/`dp3`) for the ten normal variants;
* ordered dither, `texkill` and dependent multi-tap reads for the dither
  variants.

The four refraction-UV shaders do not write a colour at all: `oC0.xy` is a
projected UV (`rcp` by `w`, `min`/`cmp` distortion clamp, vertex addend).  TEV
cannot emit `rcp` or an arbitrary varying addend, so the plan writes the flat
no-distortion centre `(0.5, 0.5, 1, 1)` and records the whole distortion path
as dropped.

## 5. `def` literals outside [0,1]

Nothing is clamped.  Every entry has `def_policy`:

```json
"def_policy": {
  "original_literals": [-8192.0, 2.0, -1.0, ...],
  "outside_unit_range": [-8192.0, 2.0, -1.0, ...],
  "treatment": "... folded into selectors / dropped with the expression ...",
  "clamped": 0
}
```

* `-8192`, `2`, `-1`, `-2` are sign-expansion constants; they fold into TEV
  and D3D selectors (`1-x`, negation, `x*2-1`) or disappear with an expression
  that the lite recipe does not keep.
* `-0.5`, `0.1`, `-0.1`, `0.9`, `-0.09`, `1.8`, `255`, `1/255` and the
  normal-blur constants are dropped with the original expression, not rewritten
  as clamped in-range values.
* The three exact sky rows no longer need a split runtime binding for
  `c0.xxxx`/`c0.yyyy`.  They now declare `constant_ops` that pack
  `c0` into derived TEV registers `c7`/`c6`; `constant_bindings` documents the
  source lane and the plan is generated by the runtime before the batch.

## 6. Stage/texture budget and `passes[]`

Every plan is 1-3 stages and at most 2 textures, well inside the 6-stage and
3-texture PICA limits.  The retail programs with >6 stages or >2 textures are
**collapsed** to the lite recipe, and the loss is recorded per hash, so
`passes[]` is not needed.  The two infrastructure escapes remain available for
future exact work.
