## 3. Water: 9 hashes translated, all approximate

One scene sample + prebaked fresnel + one additive highlight; **reflection is
not a real reflection pass**. The two-render-target refraction/reflection pair
is deliberately not reproduced on PICA.

| recipe | 2 stages | hashes | what is dropped |
|---|---|---|---|
| `water_surface_fresnel_lite` | `TEX0 x PRIMARY`, then `+ const highlight`; alpha 1 | `44248bf6`, `e9e6f6a8`, `92fb4434`, `2895c53c`, `497a6e33` | normal map, `dp3`/`rcp`/`pow` fresnel, 2-3 light loop, specular |
| `water_surface_cube_lite` | `CUBE x PRIMARY`, then `+ const highlight`; alpha 1 | `3e6befd4`, `0f008dd5` | per-pixel reflection vector, normal map, fresnel, specular |
| `water_fog_lerp_lite` | `INTERPOLATE(TEX0, const fog, const.a=0.9)`; alpha `TEX0.a` | `71e129a0`, `1a48a3c1` | 3-colour `cmp` gradient replaced by one fog colour; `texkill` left to an external alpha test |

Visual price, in order of visibility:

1. **Water**: no true refraction/reflection pair and no per-pixel normal
   perturbation. Fresnel becomes a vertex-prebaked tint; the sun is one constant
   add. Expect flat, uniformly tinted water with a static highlight rather than
   moving caustic sparkle. This is the stated port-lite trade, not a clamp.
2. **Clouds**: the two-texture lerp is exact, but the scroll rate is not in the
   descriptor; it must be baked into the frame constants by the runtime.
3. **Sky sprites**: `fa8e` loses the second-UV fade, `013ad` loses the 1.5 gain
   (TEV scale is 1/2/4/0.5 only). `013ad`'s brightness drop is the largest
   single-shader delta.
4. **Fog**: one colour instead of a three-colour height/depth gradient; banding
   is replaced by a flat tint.

## 4. `def` literals and constants

* `0`, `1`, `-1` and the `-8192`/`2`/`-1` sign-expansion constants are folded
  into TEV selector semantics or discarded where the expression proves they are
  identities.
* Non-trivial material constants are **not** clamped into the descriptor. They
  are named TEV constant slots: `constants` lists the slots and the extra
  `constant_bindings` field states which IR scalar feeds each slot (for example
  `{"0": {"rgb": "c0.y"}, "1": {"alpha": "c0.x"}}`).
* The three exact rows require that split binding; the runtime must feed two TEV
  constant colours from one retail register. This is recorded as a
  precondition, not silently approximated.
* The 39 census `def_literal_out_of_range` hashes are **not** blocked only by
  the literal range: inspection shows `cmp`/`rcp`/loops in the same programs.
  Rolling the range alone would still refuse most of them.

## 5. Shared requests (not edited here)

1. **Planner**: teach `tools/shaders/compile.py` to (a) merge multiple `oC0`
   partial writes into one stage, (b) lower `lrp` to `INTERPOLATE`, and (c)
   support two constant slots per stage. That would make `91ab`/`250481`/`37902`
   generator-reproducible instead of hand-written.
2. **Schema**: add an explicit `constant_bindings`/uniform-bank object and an
   `alpha_test` field. This pack encodes both today as extra fields plus
   preconditions, which `plan_verify` tolerates but does not validate.
3. **`asm.py`/`compile.py` follow-up from phase 1** (relative tuples,
   `texldp`/`texldb`, no-destination opcodes) is still required before the
   refraction `texldp` family can be claimed.
4. No change to `DevCaps`; no frame claim.

* coverage: 45 hashes partition into 14 plans + 31 refusals, 82 chunks add up;

* plan vs IR (when `E:\host_sm3\ps_ir.bin` exists): instruction counts, sampler

  `lrp` form);
